# Godolphin Barb — Part 3: affection and love

> The third AI. Where Byerley orders the abstract and Darley grounds it on real
> hardware as one representation, Godolphin **meets each device on its own terms** —
> tending to the contexts a single mapping can't serve. Love is the right register:
> she doesn't force one form on every screen, she gives each the form it deserves.

Project frame and shared grounding: [grand-masters.md](grand-masters.md). Builds on
[byerley-turk.md](byerley-turk.md) (the structure) and
[darley-arabian.md](darley-arabian.md) (the display reality that tells her *when* one
representation has run out).

(In-game the horse is the "Godolphin **Barb**" — the historical alternate naming, not
"Arabian".)

## Thesis

Byerley + Darley deliver one coherent representation that maps honestly onto any
display. But some displays are too far out for that single form to serve well — a
phone in portrait, touch instead of pointer, an ultrawide that wants a different
distribution. Godolphin owns the decision to show a **different representation** there,
and the alternate surface forms themselves. **Device-specific representation is hers.**

## Deliverables (firm)

1. **Device-specific surface forms.** When a context needs a *different* form — not
   just Darley's rescaled single mapping — Godolphin owns it: the real phone layout
   (deliberate portrait surfaces, not collapsed desktop chrome), touch ergonomics, and
   any other per-device representation. This retires the Make Debut phone-containment
   stopgap (drawer killed below 620px, bar stacked) and replaces it with an intended
   mobile experience.

2. **The swap decision.** Where the system chooses one representation over another,
   reading Darley's view/viewport facts ("what can be displayed") to decide when the
   single mapping has run out and an alternate form takes over.

## Non-goals (the seam to the other sires)

- **The single representation.** The abstract glass structure (Byerley) and its honest
  px mapping onto any display (Darley) are not Godolphin's to redefine — she engages
  only where that single representation stops serving.
