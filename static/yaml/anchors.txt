## Holiday anchors (consolidated JP + EN source of truth)
## Each `start:` is a fully-qualified ISO timestamp (date + time + offset).
## Drop-time conventions encoded in those timestamps:
##   New Year:    05:00 JST (early release for temple visits — a JP-server exception)
##   Golden Week: 12:00 JST (standard JP content drop)
##   EN:          22:00 UTC (canonical "appeared on Global" instant)
##
## These are `anchor-*` points: span-0 launches that anchored events (in
## static/anchored.yaml) hang their lead-ins / extensions off. The point itself
## carries only its date(s) and any curated login-bonus rewards — display names
## (Golden Week's themed title) ride on the anchored spans, not here.


## Anchored events (curated lead-ins / extensions around a tentpole)
##
## Each entry pins itself to one *edge* of another event (the `anchor`) and
## runs for `duration` (ISO-8601, e.g. P7D, PT12H — weeks/days/hours/min/sec;
## years/months are rejected since they don't map to a fixed span). The key
## prefix is load-bearing and sets the direction:
##
##   before-*  ends   AT the anchor's start, running `duration` *before*  it
##   after-*   starts AT the anchor's end,   running `duration` *after*   it
##   during-*  synonym for after- — reads better for a part that runs *during*
##             a multi-part celebration; resolves to the same placement
##
## To add an event: copy a block below, rename the key with the right prefix,
## point `anchor` at the launch (or the prior part, to chain), set `duration`.
##
## The anchor is the stable key of a base launch (anchor-new-year-*,
## anchor-golden-week-*, anchor-anni-*, scenario-*) OR of another anchored
## event — chaining. An `after-`
## chain tiles contiguously: each link begins exactly where its parent ended.
## Periods (JST, and EN derived later) are computed from the anchor's; nothing
## here is per-region. Resolution fails loud if an anchor can't be reached.
