The horsetrader **account overlay** (snapshot + income config) is deliberately
shaped like the **Henry Handsome Derby's Carat Calculator** — the community's
de-facto planning Google Sheet. Same inputs: Team Trial / Club / Champion's Meeting
/ League of Heroes tiers, Daily Carat Pack, Training Pass, monthly income,
crystal/shard balances, per-banner carat estimates + MLB odds. Rationale: planners
have **already been taught these concepts**, so the surface needs no new vocabulary
— principle 5's "meet them where they are" one level up (borrowing the community's
learned *planning model*, not just the game's visual grammar). Makes the "edge over
a spreadsheet" framing literal: *that* is the spreadsheet; horsetrader is its
better-UX, true-to-date successor.

The calculator also carries **League of Heroes** + **Training Pass** rows (dated
"Implemented Jan/Aug 2027" as Global catches up) — config rows horsetrader will
likely grow into, but [[project_league_of_heroes_todo]] is still ETL-unmodelled.

Captured in docs/frontend/ui.md (`## The account overlay`). See
[[project_ui_design_doc]].
