**I want to be thrilled! An informant seeking the unknown.**

Transcend is an information-savvy Umamusume who loves delving into subcultures. She has a keen eye for the latest gadgets and her curiosity about what's new and cool always thrills her everyday. An excellent communicator, Transcend has the ability to pop into any group and join their conversation, hungry for the information she might find out.
