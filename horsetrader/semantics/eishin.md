# Eishin Flash (eishin)

[umamusu.wiki/Eishin_Flash](https://umamusu.wiki/Eishin_Flash)

> "Remain rigorous and exact in all that you do, and never stray from the recipe."

**Everything she does is according to her plan. A solemn and diligent mädchen.**

A diligent and methodical exchange student from Germany. Everything she does is planned an scheduled down to the second. She likes to bake sweets as a hobby, and her precise nature may have arisen due to the unforgiving nature of measuring errors and baking. However, this same meticulousness makes her quick to panic when unexpected events of issues arrive.[1]
