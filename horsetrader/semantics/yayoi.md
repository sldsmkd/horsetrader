# Akikawa Yayoi (yayoi)

> "Greetings! I hereby welcome you to Tracen Academy! Now, go forth, and make your Umamusume's dreams come true!"

**Director of Tracen Academy — the patron whose out-of-pocket generosity keeps the students supplied.**

Behold! Akikawa is the director of Tracen Academy. Behind her exuberant tone and behavior lies a strong will to support all Umamusume. Her generous out-of-pocket support has earned her the respect and gratitude of the students, as well as some stern talking-tos from her secretary Tazuna.
