# Matikanefukukitaru (Fuku-chan)

[umamusu.wiki/Matikanefukukitaru](https://umamusu.wiki/Matikanefukukitaru)

> "Oho, what's this I see? Today's divination: great fortune!"

**Fortune be with me! This charm-tastic Umamusume loves fortune-telling.**

A fortune-telling fanatic and lover of the supernatural, she's content to leave the outcomes of races and many other things up to luck. If she's not divining for her own sake, then she's divining for someone else's. She places an immense faith in a revelation from a certain god stating that as long as she keeps running, the way forward will open up before her. The name of the cat shaped bag that she carries is "Miss Nya."
