# Zenno Rob Roy (robroy)

[umamusu.wiki/Zenno_Rob_Roy](https://umamusu.wiki/Zenno_Rob_Roy)

> "To be like the hero of a fantastical tale... I can't imagine any book lover not wishing the same."

**One day, she'll write her own epic tale. A voracious reader with a rich imagination.**

A bibliophile who serves as a student library assistant at Tracen Academy. Though she comes off as quiet and reserved, within her burns the desire to embody the hero of an epic tale. She had always overcome most hurdles with ease, but rarely stood out. Ever since winning a certain freestyle race, however, the humble dream of becoming like the hero Rob Roy has welled up within her.
