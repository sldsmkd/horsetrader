## Goal
Every reward producer is a registry entry with: a namespaced identity, a uniform control surface (enabled + optional stream-owned scale), and its business logic co-located. No producer wired outside the registry. No exceptions.

## A. Namespacing (do first — everything else references it)
- Dotted hierarchy, Java/DNS-style, general→specific: **`flow.selector.name`**. Readable, self-grouping (`income.play.*`).
  - **flow** — `income` | `spend` (adds or removes resources; `spend.*` grows as client spend strategies land).
  - **selector** — what drives it: `ground` | `play` | `identity` | `subscription` | `commitment` (audit column 4; maps 1:1 to the control surface — `play.*` are the sweetie/sweaty-scaled ones).
  - **name** — the producer.
- Namespace ≠ capability. Taxonomy (this) and capability (late-ness, §E — the only capability distinction left) are orthogonal — never merge them.
- Stream ids name STREAMS; stable keys name EVENTS (and hence emissions — `source` = the event's key, §E). The old idea of deriving emission sources by going deeper in the dotted tree is superseded: sub-source attribution falls out of minted event keys (`routine-dailies-<date>`), killing the `daily-pack`/`daily-carats` mismatch and the scattered `*_SOURCE` consts the same way, with one naming system instead of two.

The full map:
```
income.ground.events           income.play.story               (GradedStamp, claims story-, §E)
income.ground.generator        income.play.champions-meeting   (GradedStamp, claims cm-, §E)
income.ground.sequence         income.identity.club-rank
income.play.missions           income.subscription.daily-pack
income.play.routine            income.subscription.training-pass
income.play.team-trials        spend.commitment.banners        (late — reads P_income, §E)
income.play.shop-tickets

(income.ground.events claims the complement; all others are claimers or synthesisers, §E)
```

## B. Control surface (the OVERVIEW contract)
- Move the on/off gate OUT of the `channels.ts` emit closures INTO the producer. Today `play.missions==="yes"?…:[]` and `if(!config)return[]` live in the registry; they should be the producer answering "am I enabled?".
- Producer surface = `enabled(ctx)` + `claims` + `events(ctx)` (§E). Scale interpretation (`DAYS_PER_WEEK`, `STORY_TIER`, …) already lives in the producer — keep it; it's the half that's already right. "Be sweetie" now answers as *which events you produce, priced how* — never as payment timing (the fold owns that).
- Frontend says "be sweetie"; the producer reaches into ctx/config and decides what that means for it.

## C. Producer shape
- The old **spec vs direct-fold** split dissolves under the events currency: both silhouettes are event sources — *claimers* (price the baked events they own) and *synthesisers* (mint events the bake doesn't carry). The distinction stays real but is stream-private implementation, not a registry dimension.
- Collapse the **single-spec `*FromBundle`→`*Stream`** pair into one producer method; the two-function dance is an artifact.
- Target: a producer is one object with one verb, not a free-function pair.

## D. Shared helpers
- One `flatPayload`, and its home is now forced: it lives in the coordinator's generic fold (§E), the one place rewards become money. Delete the private copies in `routine.ts` and `championsmeeting.ts`; streams never touch payment.

## E. Streams of events, not rewards (+ the late wall)
_REVISED 2026-06-12 (twice — the morning version supersedes the overnight hooks model). **The currency inversion: streams produce EVENTS, not rewards.** Rewards are a property of events; money is a fold over them; cards are a view over them. One currency, two projections — the ledger and the lane. The old vocabulary ("income channels", emissions-as-output) named the derived thing and kept pulling the design toward it._
- **One `Stream` = `id` + `enabled(ctx)` + `claims` + `events(ctx) → list[Event]`.** One verb. Whether a stream stamps (CM, stories), filters, synthesises (routine, sequence), or passes baked records through untouched is stream-private — no reshape/emit hook pair, no phases, no coordinator introspection. Streams are mutually anonymous: indifferent to each other's existence, period (the hooks model only achieved indifference-to-authorship).
- **Partition by stable key.** `claims` = declared key prefixes (`("cm-",)`, `("story-",)`); stable keys were *designed* routable ("split on the first `-`", stable_key.py). Disjointness is asserted at registry construction — the double-pay foot-gun becomes a constructor-time impossibility, not a test. `income.ground.events` claims the complement (the live missions/events split is the precedent). Uniqueness inside a claim is the bake's msgspec-map guarantee: trust the bake, a collision is a bug.
- **Synthesisers mint keys** under prefixes the bake never uses, derived from the stream's identity (`routine-dailies-<date>`). Synthesised cadence events are `visible: false` (existing opt-out machinery) — on the ledger, off the lane.
- **Coordinator = assert-partition → union → one generic fold.** The union of stream outputs is the settled world: simultaneously the render source (cards = its visible events, reward faces included — "what am I rendering?" answered by construction) and the fold input. Money derivation exists ONCE, in the fold: pay at `end`, `start` when rushed, `flatPayload` the rewards. Stories' duplicated rushed-timing isn't fixed, it's *unwritable* — no stream computes payment timing.
- **Emission `source` = the event's stable key.** Sub-source attribution falls out of minted keys; §A's parallel dotted-source scheme dies — one naming system (stream ids name streams; stable keys name events/emissions).
- **Bake immutable; the settled world lives one fold.** `events(ctx)` is a pure derivation re-run from pristine on every rebuild (update→fold→notify→refresh). Re-derivability, not reversibility; never derive from a derived world.
- **Streams COMMUTE — purchased, not assumed.** Disjoint claims make the event layer order-blind (union of disjoint sets); the constructor's partition assertion is the proof obligation. The money layer commutes iff **the fold stays linear**: `balance = base + Σ deltas`, no clamping/min-max/balance-conditionals in the fold ever (negative free carats stay allowed; flooring belongs inside a spend strategy's own pass). Registry order means nothing; whole-stream gating is sound (removing a summand can't perturb the others); per-stream incremental refold becomes legal for free. Ordering is real only INSIDE the late pass (spends sorts its own banners earlier-by-start) — sequence matters within a spend strategy, never between streams.
- **The late wall survives every revision because the currency genuinely changes there.** A spend is not an event — it's a claim against the fold's result. `LateStream`: `emit(ctx, income) → emissions`; P_income does not exist until the fold has run. Spends, N=1. The one money-native citizen.
- **`GradedStamp`** (shared rule, N=2): an event-source that returns its claimed events priced — select the play-graded `reward_maps` row, stamp onto own events; `row(ctx, event)` is the sole varying part (CM's map key fixed; stories' reads `event.era`). CM + stories now; the parked PvP trio (#61) are occupants 3–5. Resolves stories' old mid-channel sins (duplicated rush timing; the card's second era×tier lookup).
- **Anchored events (`before-`/`after-`) are the one key family that breaks prefix routing** (first token is a relation, not a type) — and the one already sentenced (#62 anchor retirement). Land #62 before/with eclipse; post-#62 invariant: every surviving prefix routes to exactly one stream.
- **Read/write split: the projection is never written — only derived.** Account state (snapshot, commitments, play, identity, subscriptions) is the write store; the fold is a pure QUERY over `(bake, account state)`, ctx frozen for its duration. Writes are user transactions at the edges (shield COMMIT, slider change, snapshot save), serialized, each followed by full re-derivation (update→fold→notify→refresh = write→re-query→refresh views). A new commitment writes ctx.commitments, never the ledger; every fold re-reads it — a previous cycle's spend is a read like any other (and a fully-spent past dissolves into the snapshot base). Commutativity and re-derivability are corollaries: all streams are reads of frozen state; the late pass is a read of a *derived* value, hence the staged P_income boundary.
- **`available` is a query, not a fold output** (RESOLVED, carries over). The shield's single-threaded BEGIN/UPDATE/COMMIT means every availability read happens against a settled world; `bannerAvailable()` re-runs the pure ordered pass on demand, self-excluding by construction.
- Pipeline with projections as boundary values: `union(streams.events) → settled world → fold → P_income → late(P_income) → P_final`. The coordinator keeps the settled world + both projections; availability queries run against **P_income** (NOT the flattened P_final — a self-excluded final-series read would wrongly deduct overlapping later-starting banners; availability is income minus strictly earlier-by-start claims).

## Sequencing
