# **Analysis of Winter Live-Ops in Umamusume Pretty Derby: Chronological Retrospective of Currency Vectors, Retention Metrics, and Progression Incentives (2021–2026)**

The winter seasonal block represents a critical high-value window in the annual live-ops cycle for top-tier mobile applications in the Japanese market. For *Umamusume Pretty Derby*, developed by Cygames, this period spans Christmas events, Year-End Countdown campaigns, and New Year celebrations.1 Over its operational lifespan from 2021 to 2026, the game has utilized a highly structured, phased campaign architecture.1 This setup is designed to stabilize Daily Active Users (DAU), incentivize progression, and drive high monetization via concurrent currency gifts and premium progression items.6

## **Structural Design of Holiday Live-Ops Cycles**

Cygames has established a highly consistent seasonal cadence divided into sequential and concurrent phases.1 The cycle typically initiates in the second week of December with a dedicated Christmas Campaign.9 This initial phase uses flat soft currency injections and thematic daily progression items to establish an active engagement baseline ahead of the primary holidays.9  
By late December, the game transitions into the "Year-End and New Year Campaign" Phase 1 (*ゆく年くる年キャンペーン第1弾*).1 This phase runs concurrently with the tail-end of the Christmas window and introduces the Year-End Countdown Login Bonus alongside significant gameplay boosts, such as a fifty percent reduction in Training Points (TP) consumption.2  
On New Year’s Day, operations transition into the main celebration window, launching the "New Year Special Login Bonus" concurrently with premium guaranteed gacha banners and livestream-associated presents.7  
By early to mid-January, Phase 2 (*第2弾*) is deployed to counter post-holiday engagement drop-offs.1 This secondary phase shifts its focus from currency injections to gameplay optimization, introducing boosted character intimacy acquisition rates, elevated item drops in competitive modes like the Team Stadium, and increased daily resource challenge attempts.3  
This progression of phases establishes a continuous, 50-day live-ops sequence that sustains retention across the year-end transition.

## **Chronological Breakdown of Christmas Campaign Dynamics**

The Christmas period serves as the baseline builder for the wider winter live-ops period. In the inaugural 2021–2022 cycle, Cygames deployed the "KIRARI MAGIC SHOW" Christmas Campaign.15 This campaign integrated a 12-day login bonus that began on December 12, 2021, at 12:00 and concluded on December 24, 2021, at 4:59, distributing 150 jewels on the initial login day.16 At the conclusion of this daily login sequence on December 24, 2021, at 5:00, a flat "Christmas Present" of 500 jewels was delivered directly to all player mailboxes, remaining claimable until December 26, 2021, at 4:59.15  
In subsequent operational years, Cygames shifted the campaign parameters to establish earlier engagement and integrate broadcast rewards. For the 2023–2024 cycle, the flat Christmas gift of 500 jewels was moved forward to December 11, 2023, at 12:00, decoupling it from the immediate Christmas Eve window to provide immediate utility for mid-month banners.17  
This early-December timing was maintained during the 2024–2025 cycle, launching on December 10, 2024, at 12:00.9 This iteration featured a flat 500-jewel present paired with a 350-jewel broadcast gift celebrating the "Paka Live TV Vol. 47" program (comprising a 150-jewel broadcast reward and a 200-jewel interactive "Pinball Derby" reward).11  
To sustain engagement throughout the month, Cygames executed concurrent Christmas Present Campaigns in two distinct phases: Phase 1 (starting December 10 at 12:00) distributed 100,000 daily game money (manny) up to a maximum of 800,000 manny, while Phase 2 (starting December 19 at 12:00) provided five daily "Toughness 30" AP-restoration items up to a maximum of 40 units.9  
The 2025–2026 cycle followed a similar format, launching on December 11, 2025, at 12:00.10 The baseline 500-jewel Christmas present was accompanied by a larger 700-jewel stream injection from "Paka Live TV Vol. 59".18 This broadcast package consisted of a 150-jewel direct login gift and 550 jewels earned during the program’s interactive segment.19

## **Chronological Breakdown of Year-End Countdown and New Year Login Bonuses**

The core of the holiday retention vector relies on the handoff between the Year-End Countdown and the New Year Special login bonuses.4 The Countdown Login Bonus operates on a daily reset cycle of 12:00 JST, while the New Year Special Login Bonus transitions to the standard 5:00 JST reset on January 1\.2

### **The 2021–2022 Inaugural Holiday Cycle**

During the game's first winter cycle, Phase 1 launched on December 27, 2021, at 12:00, introducing a five-day "Year-End Countdown Login Bonus" that remained active until January 1, 2022, at 4:59.2 This sequence provided a daily injection of 150 jewels, rewarding active players with a total of 750 jewels.2  
Exactly at the transition reset on January 1, 2022, at 5:00, the "New Year Special Login Bonus" launched, remaining active until January 28, 2022, at 4:59.3 This login bonus distributed 4,200 jewels over 10 days, using a front-loaded structure that awarded 1,500 jewels on day one and 300 jewels on each subsequent day.3

### **The 2022–2023 Condensed Holiday Cycle**

The 2022–2023 transition operated on a compressed live-ops schedule.1 Phase 1 did not commence until December 29, 2022, at 12:00, resulting in a shortened three-day countdown that concluded on January 1, 2023, at 4:59.22 This shortened window reduced the maximum countdown reward to 450 jewels, distributed at 150 jewels per day.1  
The main "New Year Special Login Bonus" ran from January 1, 2023, at 5:00 to January 31, 2023, at 4:59.22 It maintained the established 10-day, 4,200-jewel maximum cap, resulting in a combined holiday login bonus of 4,650 jewels for players who participated in both phases.22

### **The 2023–2024 Transverse Holiday Cycle and Anime Integration**

For the 2023–2024 cycle, the campaign calendar was adjusted back to a four-day countdown running from December 28, 2023, at 12:00 to January 1, 2024, at 4:59, which yielded 600 total jewels at 150 jewels per day.6 This countdown was supplemented by a year-end stream present from the "Meca Derby Year-End Grand Prix" broadcast, which distributed a flat gift of 150 jewels to all registered players.13  
The main "New Year Special Login Bonus" launched on January 1, 2024, at 5:00 and ran until January 31, 2024, at 4:59, distributing 4,200 jewels over 10 days.13 This brought the combined seasonal login bonus to 4,800 jewels.26

### **The 2024–2025 Holiday Cycle and Progression Monetization**

The 2024–2025 campaign launched on December 27, 2024, at 12:00.4 It featured a five-day countdown ending on January 1, 2025, at 4:59, distributing 750 total jewels.4  
The subsequent "New Year Special Login Bonus" ran from January 1, 2025, at 5:00 to January 31, 2025, at 4:59, delivering the standard 4,200 jewels over 10 days of login.4 The combined total from these two sequential login bonuses reached 4,950 jewels.28

### **The 2025–2026 Era-Peak Holiday Cycle and Calendar Dilations**

The 2025–2026 cycle featured an expanded countdown window due to holiday calendar positioning.8 Phase 1 was deployed on December 26, 2025, at 12:00, establishing a six-day countdown that concluded on January 1, 2026, at 4:59.8 This sequence yielded a record countdown reward of 900 jewels.14  
The subsequent "New Year Special Login Bonus" ran from January 1, 2026, at 5:00 to January 30, 2026, at 4:59, distributing 4,200 jewels over 10 days.8 Combined, these login bonuses provided players with a total of 5,100 jewels over the holiday transition period.  
Additionally, players received a 500-jewel stream present on January 1, 2026, associated with the "Paka Live TV Vol. 60" broadcast (consisting of a 150-jewel login gift and 350 jewels from the interactive "Pinball Derby" segment).8

## **Premium Incentive Vectors and Advanced Progression Mechanics**

A key feature of the winter campaign structure is the distribution of high-value aesthetic and progression-based items.2 These items are designed to drive engagement for veteran players and provide accessible entry points for newcomers.5 Over the five-year operational history of *Umamusume Pretty Derby*, these rewards have transitioned from cosmetic distributions to advanced, utility-driven progression packs.2

### **Cosmetic Distributions**

In the 2021–2022 cycle, Cygames used the winter campaign to distribute a flat cosmetic reward.2 On December 27, 2021, at 12:00, all players were gifted the "トレセン学園制服（冬）" (winter Trecen school uniforms).2 This cosmetic item was made permanently available for all characters in the game’s home screen and concert modes, serving as a high-visibility, zero-cost retention gift.2

### **Thematic Paid Selector Mechanics**

The 2023–2024 cycle introduced a significant transition in monetization via the "TV Anime Season 3 Commemorative Jewel Pack".6 Released on January 1, 2024, at 12:00, this paid bundle packaged 1,500 paid jewels with a "★3 Exchange Ticket" (Selector).6  
This selector allowed players to bypass the standard gacha pool and choose any 3-star character implemented up to December 20, 2023, serving as a powerful monetization vector targeted at players looking to round out their competitive rosters.6

### **Hard Progression Materials**

During the 2024–2025 and 2025–2026 cycles, Cygames expanded its monetization options by offering paid progression materials.5 On December 27, 2024, at 12:00, the game launched the "Factor Reinforcement Report Pack" (*因子強化レポートパック*) for 1,500 paid jewels.7 This bundle contained the "Factor Reinforcement Report," an item that allowed players to select a Hall of Fame character and directly upgrade their inherited stats or skill factors.7  
This progression pack was reintroduced in the 2025–2026 cycle on December 26, 2025, at 12:00.5 To prevent hoarding and maintain the long-term progression balance, the report was issued with a strict expiration date of February 2, 2026, at 11:59, ensuring that the item was consumed within the active seasonal window.5

## **Strategic Implications for Live-Ops and Retention Architecture**

An analysis of these winter campaigns reveals several key operational trends that highlight Cygames' approach to managing its live-ops and monetization lifecycle.

### **Optimization of Calendar Alignment**

The variation in the Year-End Countdown Login Bonus durations (spanning from three to six days) shows how Cygames actively adapts its campaigns to calendar positioning.1 Rather than adhering to a rigid year-end calendar, the developers stretch the countdown window when year-end holidays start early (as seen in 2025).8 This allows them to capture early holiday engagement and maintain DAU stability during the critical transition into the New Year.14

### **Programmatic Control of Currency Value**

The New Year Special Login Bonus has remained set at exactly 4,200 jewels over 10 days across all five operational years.4 This consistency helps prevent soft-currency inflation, ensuring that the basic value of currency in the game remains stable year over year.8  
To augment this baseline, Cygames uses high-value, non-currency gifts (such as daily free 10-pull gacha campaigns, broadcast rewards, and progression packs) to drive seasonal engagement without over-inflating the player base's primary jewel reserves.1

### **Segmented Monetization Models**

The progression of winter monetization formats demonstrates a clear shift toward targeted and utility-driven spending.5 Initially, the game relied on broad, release-year-specific guaranteed gacha banners.3 Over time, these offerings were refined, first by dividing the banners into biannual release windows to reduce pool dilution, and then by introducing specialized zodiac-themed banners (such as the Year of the Snake and Year of the Horse gachas).7  
This specialization, combined with premium progression items like the Factor Reinforcement Report, allows Cygames to segment its player base.7 While casual players can log in and enjoy a stable supply of free currency and gacha pulls 2, high-spending veterans are provided with direct, paid progression paths to optimize their competitive rosters, sustaining both long-term retention and steady revenue generation.5
