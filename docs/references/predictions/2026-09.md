# Forecast check — September 2026 cycle

Frozen **2026-08-26**, before the September Cygames EN release-schedule
announcement. Our forecast is from `static/json/events.json` at `e4a0efc`;
that file had no working-tree diff at capture time. The comparison forecast is
a best-effort transcription of two uma.moe planner screenshots supplied on the
same date.

This is the pre-registration, not a score. Fill in the announced windows after
the official image arrives, without changing the frozen columns below.

Windows are calendar dates. Our records carry exact UTC instants (normally a
22:00 start); the table omits those times so they can be compared with
uma.moe's date-labelled planner. In the delta column, `+3/+2` means uma.moe's
start is three days and its end is two days later than ours.

uma.moe's `+N more` labels are retained where they are useful evidence, but
their planner can count lower-rarity or collapsed entries beyond the principal
pickups emitted by our bake. A spelling difference such as our **Shuka Sho**
versus uma.moe's **Shuuka Sho** is also preserved rather than silently
normalised.

## Pre-announcement read

The forecasts agree on the event sequence and all principal banner contents in
the shared horizon. Timing is the contest: excluding Queen Elizabeth II Cup,
uma.moe places every shared start on or after ours, usually **2–5 days later**.
Japan Cup is the widest visible start gap at **6 days**. Queen Elizabeth II Cup
is the lone reversal, with uma.moe one day earlier.

That directional split is worth retaining after the result: August's frozen
record found recent confirmed EN dates running later than the full-history
all-event line, but also warned that the anniversary period can locally distort
the cadence. September's official schedule will provide the next clean test of
whether our earlier line or uma.moe's later placement is closer.

## Scorecard

This scores the **13 shared rows for which the official schedule supplies both
endpoints**. Errors are calendar-date differences, matching the frozen planner
labels: `prediction - official`, so a negative value is early. Each start and
end counts once. A printed 21:59 close and a modelled whole-minute boundary on
the same date are therefore the same endpoint; this neither adds an inclusive
day nor scores a one-minute cutoff difference as a day. For the per-event
result, the lower sum of absolute start and end error wins. Holiday Celebration
Part 1 is excluded because it has neither a forecast nor a published endpoint.
Unmentioned missions and reruns are not treated as false positives because
this schedule does not settle them.

| Metric | Horsetrader | uma.moe |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Start-date mean absolute error | **1.15 days** | 2.08 days |
| End-date mean absolute error | 1.15 days | **1.08 days** |
| All-endpoint mean absolute error | **1.15 days** | 1.58 days |
| Median absolute start/end error | **1d / 1d** | 2d / 1d |
| Exact date-labelled windows | **2 of 13** | 0 of 13 |
| Per-event window wins | **9** | 2 |

Two further rows tied. Horsetrader's exact windows were Shuka Sho and Nakayama
Festa. uma.moe won the Wonder Acute and Narita Top Road/Eishin Flash windows:
it got both September 23 starts exactly, while ours placed that pair three days
early.

The directional biases explain the result. Horsetrader was consistently early:
its signed mean was **−1.15 days** for both starts and ends. uma.moe was late by
**+2.08 days** on starts and **+0.62 days** on ends. That made uma.moe very
slightly better at predicting closes, but Horsetrader's much stronger starts
and two exact windows gave it the lower overall error and the **9–2 event win,
with 2 ties**.

## Takeaway

**Horsetrader was more accurate for September.** The earlier timing line was
usually the right side of the disagreement: through September 15 we were exact
or one day early, while uma.moe was commonly one to four days late. Our clear
miss was the September 23 banner pair, where uma.moe's later placement was
right. Both forecasts then bracketed the September 28 Christmas-cluster start,
with ours one day early and uma.moe three days late.
