**Legend-race fix (this session).** The first re-bake ran the June Legend Race
10 days (Jun 8 – 18); actual is 6 (Jun 7 – 13). Cause: the predictor used a
single `mean(confirmed EN spans)`, but **every** EN-confirmed pair predates the
mid-2022 cut from 3–4 legs to 2 legs (`legendrace-013` on), so the mean froze
the old ~10d era onto the 2-leg races. Fix: span is now `ceil(that race's own JP
window)` — confirmed pairs prove EN span == ceil(JP span) exactly
(8d16:59→9d, 11d16:59→12d), so a 2-leg 5d16:59 window yields the correct 6d.
The June race that shipped (tamamo-cross + meisho-doto = `legendrace-013`) is
*itself* the first 2-leg race — the pattern break it was missing.

**Residual nits (both sub-day, accepted):** Legend Race start −1 day (DateMapper
interpolation); Cancer Cup end +1 day (CM predictor's tail runs one day long —
same shape just fixed on Legend Races, lower priority since CMs are fixed
monthly anchors).
