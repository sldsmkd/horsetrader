# Forecast check — October 2026 cycle

Frozen **2026-09-25**, before the October Cygames EN release-schedule
announcement. Our forecast is from the current `static/json/events.json`
(`sha256: 9c18f0bc1f922eccaa4cfc1c4df856cadbe1b612e195c62b57a8d79b44e3868f`)
in the working tree at `58c1f7d`. The comparison forecast is a best-effort
transcription of two uma.moe planner screenshots supplied on the same date.

This is the pre-registration, not a score. Fill in the announced windows after
the official image arrives, without changing the frozen columns below.

Windows are calendar dates. Our records carry exact UTC instants (normally a
22:00 start); the table omits those times so they can be compared with
uma.moe's date-labelled planner. In the delta column, `+3/+2` means uma.moe's
start is three days and its end is two days later than ours.

The screenshots are viewport captures, not an export. Lane totals and
`+N more` labels show that some cards or pickups are collapsed or outside the
visible area. Rows absent from the transcription must therefore not be scored
as uma.moe false negatives. Lead-pickup differences are also preserved rather
than silently normalised.

## Pre-announcement read

The two forecasts again agree on the principal character, support, story,
Legend Race, and Champions Meeting sequence. The timing disagreement changes
direction during the month rather than following September's mostly one-sided
split.

For the early Fall G1 mission carryover, uma.moe is markedly later: Japan Cup
is seven days later and Champions Cup ten days later, while the two visible
early Part 3 missions are six days later at the start. The banner forecasts
then converge around the October 18–19 New Year cluster. After that crossover,
uma.moe moves earlier: Arima Kinen Legend Race is two days earlier, and the
Hokko Tarumae pair and Capricorn Cup start one to two days before ours.

Across the 19 shared visible rows, uma.moe starts 13 later, five earlier, and
one on the same date as Horsetrader. That count is descriptive only: the
October announcement will determine which side of each disagreement was
closer.
