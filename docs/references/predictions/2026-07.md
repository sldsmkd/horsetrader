The July forecast was frozen **2026-06-15**, after curating the June schedule
but before adding any July schedule dates. The original snapshot and scoring
survived inside [`2026-06.md`](2026-06.md); this file gives July a standalone
record in the monthly series. The official source is the Cygames EN
[`2026_07.jpg`](../announcements/2026_07.jpg) release-schedule image.

Official windows below are UTC. Printed 9:59 p.m. closes are compared with the
bake's whole-minute boundary as the same calendar endpoint. Start error is
`prediction - official`, so a negative value means our forecast was early.

Memory; Copano Rickey + Manhattan Cafe/Seiun Sky; Smart Falcon + Light

## Scorecard

- **Content identity/order:** 16 of 16 modelled official releases correct. All
  10 banner identities, both story ordinals, both anniversary parts, Leo Cup,
  and Grand Live were routed to the right July slots. Anniversary Part 3 is
  intentionally outside the model and scoring.
- **Exact starts:** 3 of 16 modelled release records: the July 2 trainee banner,
  support banner, and story. Counting the combined marketing rows instead, 2
  of 11 modelled rows start exactly.
- **Near starts (within ±2 days):** 11 of 16 modelled release records. The five
  exceptions are the Bamboo Memory trainee/support pair (+5d each) and the July
  27 trainee banner, support banner, and story (−3d each).
- **Start-date error:** mean absolute error **2.0 days** across the 16 modelled
  release records; median absolute error **2 days**. Signed mean **−0.5 days**,
  so the forecast leaned slightly early overall.
- **Full-window exactness:** 3 of the 13 records for which the image supplies
  both endpoints (the July 2 banner pair and story). Leo Cup got its end
  exactly; every other miss shifted both endpoints by the same amount, so
  durations were preserved.

## Takeaway

The predictor got the hard semantic part strikingly right: release order,
co-release grouping, banner casts, stories, Champions Meeting, and scenario all
survived the reveal. Date accuracy was strongest on the July 2 story cluster,
degraded on the July 7 Bamboo cluster, recovered to within one or two days
through July 22, then ran three days early on the July 27 story cluster.

The anniversary anchor was the clearest systematic miss. We placed the
anniversary and Grand Live launch on July 20, while the official Part 2 and new
scenario celebration began July 22. That two-day error propagated intact to
both scenario banners, and the July 27 story cluster landed one additional day
early.
